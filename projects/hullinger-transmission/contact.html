Quick Questions

Before you send the quote.

What if I don't know which transmission I have?

That's fine. Send the VIN and vehicle information. We can use that to help identify the correct transmission application.

What if I don't know whether I need a rebuild or replacement?

Select “Other / not sure” and describe the symptoms. We can help compare the appropriate options for the vehicle.

Can I request a remanufactured transmission quote?

Yes. Complete remanufactured transmission replacement is available for many vehicles and can include nationwide warranty options.

Is text faster than the form?

Texting is often the quickest way to send a VIN, photos, videos, and basic vehicle information. The form is useful when you want to organize everything in one request.
