Built Beyond a Basic Rebuild

A reman engine is restored through a controlled process.

For every engine we quote, Integrity verifies the applicable remanufacturing program and written warranty. The goal is a known, repeatable standard—not an unknown-mileage engine removed from another vehicle and sold with its history still inside it.

01

Complete Teardown

The engine is disassembled and critical castings, oil passages and reusable hard parts are cleaned for inspection.

02

Precision Inspection

Blocks, heads, crankshafts, camshafts and connecting rods are checked for condition and dimensional accuracy as applicable.

03

Wear Parts & Machining

Wear items are replaced and qualified components are machined or reconditioned to the program's controlled specifications.

04

Run-Tested Quality

Supported complete assemblies receive recorded operating checks such as oil pressure, temperature, vacuum, compression and leak inspection.

Know the warranty first

Your quote identifies the exact time and mileage term, parts-and-labor coverage, installation and maintenance requirements, exclusions and claim steps for that engine. Coverage varies by application and use, so the written warranty controls.

Why Fitment Needs More Than Displacement

The same vehicle can use more than one engine configuration.

Model year and engine size are useful, but they are not always enough. Emissions certification, fuel system, sensor layout, calibration, casting changes and production date can change the correct replacement.

We use the VIN first and request the engine code, emissions label or photos only when the application needs another verification step. The final quote identifies the assembly type, included components and anything that must transfer from the original engine.

Before You Buy

Common reman engine questions.

What does the quoted engine include?

Contents vary. Your written quote identifies the assembly type and included components; do not assume accessories, manifolds, sensors or fuel-system parts are included unless listed.

Can I ship to my home?

Some freight options support residential liftgate delivery, but a qualified commercial address is normally simpler and less expensive. The actual address type is quoted before payment.

How does the core return work?

The quote lists the refundable core deposit, eligible core condition, return deadline and freight procedure. A refund is issued after the returned core is processed and accepted under those terms.
