Before You Order

Common reman transmission questions.

Here are the details customers most often want to know before placing an order.

Can the VIN identify the exact transmission I need?

The VIN identifies your vehicle and narrows the correct transmission application. We refresh the match before checkout and complete a final fitment review after payment. If we cannot supply the correct unit, we issue a full refund.

Can a remanufactured transmission be shipped overnight?

Same-day outbound shipping and expedited freight may be available for an in-stock application, but overnight delivery is not guaranteed. Delivery timing depends on inventory, destination, carrier and address type and is confirmed in the written quote.

How does the transmission core return work?

The core deposit is paid with the order. Return the correct, complete core in the supplied freight container within 30 days of delivery. The deposit is refunded after the core is received, processed and accepted under the written core terms. A wrong, incomplete, disassembled, damaged or late core may receive reduced or no credit.

Does the unit include a torque converter and fluid?

Package contents vary by transmission. Each available option lists what is included, and we confirm the components and installation requirements before payment.

Who installs a nationwide order?

You select a qualified local installer unless Integrity has quoted a Springfield-area coordinated installation. Programming, cooler service, fluid, documentation and other warranty requirements must be completed exactly as the written terms require.

Does this page show the price I will pay?

The VIN lookup shows the transmission price and refundable core deposit. Enter the delivery address to compare freight options. Applicable tax is added before payment.
