Choose the upgrade for how the vehicle is actually used.

Only upgrade levels currently offered for the VIN-matched application appear in the live results. This guide explains the remanufacturer's general selection logic for towing, severe duty and added horsepower.

  • Ordinary stock use may not need an upgrade.

Before You Order

Common reman transmission questions.

The written quote and warranty always control the exact package, responsibilities and coverage.

Can the VIN identify the exact transmission I need?

The VIN identifies important vehicle information, but public VIN data does not reliably prove every transmission interchange, calibration or production split. Integrity verifies the exact unit with the supplier before price and payment are finalized.

Can a remanufactured transmission be shipped overnight?

Same-day outbound shipping and expedited freight may be available for an in-stock application, but overnight delivery is not guaranteed. Delivery timing depends on inventory, destination, carrier and address type and is confirmed in the written quote.

How does the transmission core return work?

The core deposit is paid with the order. Return the correct, complete core in the supplied freight container within 30 days of delivery. The deposit is refunded after the core is received, processed and accepted under the written core terms. A wrong, incomplete, disassembled, damaged or late core may receive reduced or no credit.

Does the unit include a torque converter and fluid?

Many complete packages include an approved torque converter and may include fluid, but contents vary by application and supplier. The written quote identifies the exact unit, included components and installation requirements.

Who installs a nationwide order?

You select a qualified local installer unless Integrity has quoted a Springfield-area coordinated installation. Programming, cooler service, fluid, documentation and other warranty requirements must be completed exactly as the written terms require.

Does this page show the price I will pay?

The VIN lookup shows Integrity's current transmission price and the separate refundable core deposit. Freight and applicable tax depend on the delivery address and are added after the final fitment and availability check, before secure payment.
