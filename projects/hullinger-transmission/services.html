Which Service Fits?

Different vehicles call for different transmission solutions.

Instead of treating every failure the same way, we consider the application, available parts, replacement availability, warranty, vehicle age, and overall repair economics.

Many Newer Vehicles

Remanufactured replacement

Often a strong option when a quality replacement transmission is available with competitive installed pricing and nationwide warranty coverage.

Rebuild-Friendly Applications

Traditional rebuild

Often appropriate for older transmissions, classic applications, and units with strong parts availability.

Already Removed

Bench rebuild

A useful option when the transmission has already been removed and only the transmission itself needs rebuilding.

Complete Transmission Service

Installation involves more than simply swapping the transmission.

Transmission longevity and proper operation can depend on fluid, cooling-system cleanliness, programming, adaptive learning, converter condition, and other supporting systems.

The exact supporting work depends on the vehicle and transmission.

Correct Fluid Modern transmissions can be highly sensitive to fluid specification and level.
Cooler & Line Service Contamination from the failed transmission should not be carried into the replacement.
Programming & Relearn Some vehicles require programming, initialization, or adaptive relearn after replacement.
Warranty Compliance Proper installation and supporting service help protect qualifying warranty coverage.

Service Questions

Common questions before scheduling.

Do you rebuild transmissions or replace them?

Both. The appropriate option depends on the vehicle, transmission, availability, repair economics, and warranty needs.

Do you install replacement transmissions?

Yes. Complete removal and installation can be included with fluids, supporting work, programming, and adaptive relearn when applicable.

Do you offer bench rebuilds?

Yes. Selected transmissions can be rebuilt when brought in already removed from the vehicle.

What information do you need for a quote?

VIN is preferred. Otherwise send the year, make, model, engine, 2WD or 4WD, and a description of the problem.

Do you offer nationwide transmission warranties?

Qualifying remanufactured packages may include one-to-three-year unlimited-mile nationwide warranty options. Exact terms depend on the transmission and warranty package.

Do you perform general automotive repair?

Yes. We accept selected brakes, steering, suspension, hubs, bearings, axles, oil changes, and other straightforward mechanical service.

Why is transmission work paid before parts are ordered?

Major transmission jobs involve substantial transmission, parts, freight, and fluid costs. Upfront payment allows those items to be purchased immediately without financing customer repairs, helping us maintain competitive pricing.

How much does transmission replacement cost?

Pricing varies by exact transmission, drivetrain configuration, freight, core requirements, fluids, programming, labor, and warranty package. Send the VIN for an application-specific quote.
