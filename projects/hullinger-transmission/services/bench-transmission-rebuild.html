Services

Bench work separates the transmission build from the vehicle labor.

A bench rebuild can reduce total cost when a capable customer or repair shop already handles removal and installation. Integrity receives the unit, disassembles and inspects it, confirms additional damage and rebuilds it to the agreed specification.

The removed transmission must still be tied to a specific vehicle and use. Year, make, model, engine, drive type, VIN and any transmission identification help prevent parts or calibration mistakes. Send the symptoms and scan results too; they may explain damage found during teardown.

Successful installation remains essential. A contaminated cooler, incorrect converter, wrong fluid level, restricted line, wiring problem or missing programming procedure can damage a properly rebuilt transmission and affect warranty coverage.

Installation Responsibility

Bench pricing does not include vehicle removal, installation, cooler service, programming or diagnosis unless the written quote says otherwise. Installation-related failures are not covered as rebuild defects.

A Clear Path

The bench-build workflow.

The core is treated as an application-specific assembly, not an anonymous transmission case.

STEP 01

Prequalify

Send identification, symptoms, intended use and photos before transporting the unit.

STEP 02

Inspect

The transmission is disassembled, cleaned and evaluated for hard-part and hydraulic damage.

STEP 03

Authorize

Any additional damage or upgrade choices are confirmed before final assembly.

STEP 04

Return

Receive the rebuilt unit with the applicable startup, installation and warranty requirements.

Common Questions

Answers before you schedule.

These answers provide a starting point. The vehicle, evidence, written quote and warranty terms control the final recommendation.

Can I bring any removed transmission for rebuilding?

No. Bench work is selective and depends on the transmission family, parts support, core condition and current workload. Send identification before transporting the unit.

Do I need to bring the torque converter?

Ask before drop-off. The converter should be replaced or properly remanufactured for most rebuilds, and the correct unit must match the application and build.

What if hard parts are damaged?

Hard-part damage is documented after teardown and may increase the final price. If damage is extensive, Integrity may recommend a different core or reman replacement.

What does the bench warranty cover?

The written Integrity bench warranty generally covers qualifying rebuild-related defects for one year/12,000 miles and requires return to Integrity. Installation, contamination, overheating and external control problems may be excluded.
