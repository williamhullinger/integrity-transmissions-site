Local Service

A focused transmission resource for the Springfield area.

Transmission work is rarely a one-price service. The same model year can use different transmissions, programming, converters and installation requirements. Integrity starts with the VIN, vehicle details and symptoms so the quote is tied to the actual application.

Remanufactured replacement is the primary solution for many modern vehicles because it can provide predictable corrections, testing and nationwide warranty options. Selective in-house and bench rebuilding remain available when the original unit is a practical candidate.

Service is appointment-based rather than walk-in. Customers receive the correct drop-off or partner location after the job and schedule are confirmed.

Before You Drive Over

Text the VIN, mileage, symptoms and whether the vehicle moves under its own power. A team member will confirm the next step and the correct appointment location.

What Matters

How local customers can work with Integrity.

The path depends on whether the vehicle is still assembled, whether the unit has already been removed and which repair option makes sense.

01

Complete Replacement

Integrity sources the correct remanufactured unit and coordinates the transmission portion of the job. Removal and installation may be arranged through an approved partner included in the quote.

  • Application and VIN verification
  • Converter and fluid planning
  • Programming or adaptive procedures when required
02

Selective Rebuilding

A rebuild begins with teardown and inspection of the original transmission. The final scope depends on the reusable hard parts, known failure points and intended vehicle use.

  • Bench and coordinated R&R options
  • Hard-part condition evaluated after teardown
  • Towing or durability upgrades when appropriate
03

Drivetrain Work

Transfer case and differential work is accepted selectively based on the unit, symptoms, parts availability and whether an in-vehicle or bench solution is appropriate.

  • Transfer cases
  • Differentials
  • Torque-converter-related planning

Common Questions

Answers before you schedule.

These answers provide a starting point. The vehicle, evidence, written quote and warranty terms control the final recommendation.

Where is Integrity located?

Integrity serves the Springfield, Missouri area through an appointment-based operating model. The correct drop-off or partner location is confirmed after the vehicle, service and schedule are reviewed.

Do I need the VIN to get a quote?

The VIN is strongly preferred because it helps identify the exact transmission and vehicle configuration. If it is unavailable, send the year, make, model, engine and whether the vehicle is two- or four-wheel drive.

Can Integrity arrange removal and installation?

Removal and installation may be coordinated through an approved partner when included in the quote. The arrangement depends on the vehicle, service and current scheduling.

Do you serve customers outside Springfield?

Yes. Integrity regularly works with customers from surrounding Southwest Missouri communities. Confirm the appointment and location before transporting a vehicle or transmission.
