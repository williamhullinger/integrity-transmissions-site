Why Nationwide Coverage Matters

What happens if your transmission fails hundreds of miles from home?

This is one of the biggest differences between an Integrity rebuild warranty and a qualifying remanufactured transmission warranty.

Our bench-build and R&R rebuild warranties are local, return-to-us warranties. If a qualifying warranty problem develops, Integrity must inspect the transmission or vehicle and perform the approved warranty work.

A qualifying remanufactured transmission can provide nationwide warranty support through the remanufacturer. That can be especially valuable for customers who travel, commute long distances, use their vehicle for work, or simply want coverage that is not tied to one repair facility.

Bench Build Warranty

We can warranty our rebuild. We cannot warranty someone else's installation.

A bench rebuild is different from a complete R&R job because the transmission is installed outside of our control.

Modern automatic transmissions depend on more than the internal transmission itself. Fluid level and specification, cooler flow, cooler and line cleanliness, electronics, programming, linkage, external controls, engine operation, and installation procedures can all affect transmission operation and durability.

If a bench-built transmission is returned with a warranty concern, we will inspect it to determine the cause of the failure. If the problem is determined to have resulted from our rebuild, qualifying repairs are handled under the applicable Integrity warranty.

If inspection determines the failure was caused by an installation issue, contamination, incorrect fluid or fluid level, inadequate cooler flow, external vehicle problem, improper setup, or another condition outside the rebuild itself, the failure is not covered by the rebuild warranty.

Protecting a Bench-Built Transmission

  • Use the correct transmission fluid
  • Set fluid level using the proper procedure
  • Flush or replace contaminated cooler components as required
  • Verify adequate cooler flow
  • Correct vehicle-side electrical or control problems
  • Complete required programming or adaptive procedures
  • Install and adjust components correctly
  • Address engine or drivetrain problems that can damage the transmission

Installation requirements vary by transmission and vehicle. Applicable requirements should be followed for the specific unit.

Warranty Responsibilities

A warranty protects against covered failures — not every possible cause of transmission damage.

Warranty terms vary by repair type and remanufacturer. The written warranty provided with the specific job controls the actual coverage. The information below is a general explanation of common warranty conditions and is not a substitute for the written warranty agreement.

Covered Failures

Qualifying failures caused by covered transmission components, workmanship, or other conditions specifically included by the applicable warranty may be eligible for repair or replacement.

Overheating & Contamination

Transmission damage caused by overheating, contaminated cooling systems, restricted cooler flow, incorrect fluid, or external contamination may not qualify as a warrantable transmission failure.

Vehicle-Side Problems

Electrical faults, programming problems, engine issues, transfer-case failures, driveline problems, improper installation, or other vehicle-side conditions can damage or affect a transmission without being caused by the transmission itself.

Modified & Performance Vehicles

Engine modifications, increased horsepower or torque, racing, competition use, oversized tires, heavy towing, or other severe-use conditions may require a transmission specifically built and warrantied for that application.

Continued Operation

Continuing to operate a vehicle after a serious transmission problem develops can cause additional damage. Customers should contact us or the applicable warranty provider when a significant problem is detected.

Written Warranty Controls

Exact terms, mileage limitations, time limits, labor reimbursement, exclusions, claim procedures, and other conditions are determined by the written warranty applicable to the specific transmission or repair.

Transmission Warranty FAQ

Common warranty questions.

Are all Integrity transmission warranties nationwide?

No. Integrity bench rebuild and R&R rebuild warranties are return-to-us warranties. Nationwide warranty coverage is available with qualifying remanufactured transmission packages backed by the remanufacturer.

How long is the warranty on a bench rebuild?

Integrity bench rebuilds include a one-year warranty, subject to the written warranty terms. Because installation is performed outside our control, installation-related or vehicle-side failures are not covered as rebuild failures.

What warranty do I get if Integrity removes and rebuilds my transmission?

Qualifying R&R rebuild jobs include an 18-month Integrity warranty. The vehicle must return to Integrity for warranty inspection and approved repair.

What warranty comes with a remanufactured transmission?

Depending on the transmission and selected package, qualifying remanufactured transmissions may include an 18-month/18,000-mile warranty or a three-year/unlimited-mile warranty.

Does a remanufactured transmission warranty cover labor?

Qualifying warranty programs can include approved labor reimbursement at $70 per hour or $125 per hour depending on the specific remanufacturer and warranty package.

Can another shop repair my Integrity rebuild under warranty?

No. Integrity rebuild warranties are return-to-us warranties. Integrity does not reimburse another repair facility to perform warranty work on an Integrity bench or R&R rebuild.

What if my bench-build transmission fails because of installation?

If inspection determines the failure resulted from improper installation, contamination, incorrect fluid, inadequate cooler service, an external vehicle problem, or another condition outside our rebuild, the failure is not covered by the rebuild warranty.

Which warranty is best if I travel?

A qualifying remanufactured transmission warranty generally provides the strongest option for customers concerned about coverage away from Southwest Missouri because nationwide warranty support and labor reimbursement may be included.
