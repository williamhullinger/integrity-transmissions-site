Quality You Can Verify

Remanufactured means inspected, restored and tested.

A qualified reman transfer case is more than a cleaned used assembly. Integrity verifies the process and warranty behind the exact application before it is offered, so you know what standards and coverage support the unit.

01

Case Inspection

The housing is cleaned and inspected for cracks, damaged threads, wear and sealing-surface problems.

02

Internal Inspection

Shafts, gears, bearings, chains, clutches and other application-specific components are checked and addressed as required.

03

Leak & Load Checks

Depending on design, validation can include pressure, leak and clutch-torque testing before final assembly.

04

Shift & Noise Testing

Completed units are function checked for shift operation, rotation and abnormal noise under the applicable reman program.

Coverage in writing

Your quote identifies the exact time and mileage term, parts-and-labor coverage, installation requirements, excluded uses and claim procedure for the transfer case. The written warranty for that unit controls.

Fitment Without Guesswork

A transfer case name alone may not identify the correct unit.

Vehicles that appear identical can use different inputs, outputs, spline counts, speed sensors, shift motors, connectors and internal calibrations. The VIN narrows the application, and the case tag or vehicle RPO may finish the match.

We confirm what comes with the unit, which external components may need to transfer, the fluid and installation requirements, and the conditions for returning the original core.

Before You Buy

Common transfer-case questions.

Why do you need the case tag if I have a VIN?

Some vehicles have multiple cases under one model and drivetrain. The tag, RPO, connector or production date can prevent a costly mismatch when the VIN alone does not resolve the variation.

Is the shift motor included?

It depends on the quoted package. The written quote identifies included external electronics and any components that must be transferred from the original unit.

Can my local shop install it?

Yes, if the installer is qualified and follows the written fluid, programming, diagnostic and documentation requirements for the warranty package.
