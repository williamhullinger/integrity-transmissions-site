GM • Chevrolet • GMC • Cadillac

GM automatic transmissions.

GM transmission families range from classic hydraulic automatics to modern electronically controlled 6-, 8-, and 10-speed units.

GM • 4-Speed

4L60E

One of GM's most recognizable electronically controlled overdrive transmissions.

Explore 4L60E →

The 4L60E became widely used in GM rear-wheel-drive cars, trucks, and SUVs through the 1990s and 2000s. It evolved from the earlier 700R4/4L60 design and added electronic shift control.

Common applications

Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra, Tahoe, Suburban, Yukon, Camaro, Corvette, S10, TrailBlazer, and many other GM platforms.

What owners commonly notice

Slipping, loss of individual gears, delayed engagement, converter problems, harsh shifts, or complete loss of movement can occur as these units age.

Have a 4L60E problem? Text your VIN →
GM • Heavy Duty

4L80E / 4L85E

Heavy-duty GM overdrive transmissions known for strength and durability.

Explore 4L80E →

The 4L80E was designed as a heavy-duty electronically controlled overdrive transmission and is closely related in architecture to the legendary TH400.

Common applications

Heavy-duty Chevrolet and GMC trucks, vans, Suburbans, commercial vehicles, towing applications, and performance swaps.

Why enthusiasts like it

Its strong internal design has made the 4L80E popular not only in work vehicles but also in high-power swaps and performance builds.

Need 4L80E pricing? Text your VIN →
GM • 6-Speed

6L80 / 6L90

Widely used modern GM six-speed transmissions found in many trucks and SUVs.

Explore 6L80 →

GM introduced the 6L80 generation as part of the move from four-speed automatics to more efficient six-speed transmissions. The 6L90 is a heavier-duty relative used in higher-load applications.

Common applications

Chevrolet Silverado, GMC Sierra, Tahoe, Yukon, Suburban, Cadillac Escalade, Camaro, and other rear-wheel-drive GM vehicles.

Repair considerations

Converter failure, internal contamination, hydraulic issues, valve-body wear, and hard-part damage can make the overall repair strategy important on these units.

Have a 6L80 or 6L90? Text your VIN →

Explore 8L90 →

The 8L90 helped GM move beyond its six-speed generation, providing a wider gear spread and additional ratios for performance and fuel economy.

Common applications

Silverado, Sierra, Tahoe, Yukon, Escalade, Corvette, Camaro, and selected other GM platforms.

Getting Accurate Transmission Pricing

The VIN is more useful than guessing the transmission.

Transmission variations can change by engine, drivetrain, model year, production configuration, and calibration. VIN-specific identification helps us quote the correct application.

If you already know the transmission model, include it—but the VIN remains the best starting point for newer vehicles.

Send us:

  • VIN
  • Year, make, and model
  • Engine if known
  • 2WD, 4WD, or AWD
  • Current transmission symptoms
  • Transmission model if known

Transmission Questions

Before you request a quote.

Do you work on transmissions that aren't listed here?

Yes. This page highlights common transmission families and is not a complete list. Send your VIN and we can check the application.

Do you rebuild every transmission shown here?

No. Some transmissions are better suited to complete remanufactured replacement, while selected units may be rebuilt.

Can you identify my transmission from the VIN?

The VIN helps identify the vehicle configuration and is usually the best starting point for determining the correct transmission application and replacement options.

Do you install replacement transmissions?

Yes. Removal and installation can be coordinated through an approved partner and included with required fluid service, programming, and adaptive relearn when applicable.
