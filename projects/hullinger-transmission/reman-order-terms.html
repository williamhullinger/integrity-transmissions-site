Reman Transmission Order & Core Return Terms

Plain-language terms for online remanufactured transmission purchases from Integrity Transmission & Drivetrain.

Effective September 3, 2026

On this page Your Order Payment & Tax Availability & Delivery Core Deposit & Return Installation & Warranty Changes, Cancellations & Refunds

1. Your VIN-matched order

Your 17-digit VIN is required for online checkout. Integrity refreshes the selected transmission application, package price, availability and freight rate before opening Stripe Checkout. Some vehicles still have factory production splits or other fitment details that require a final review.

Payment starts that final review; it does not authorize Integrity to ship a transmission that we cannot verify as the correct application. If we cannot supply the correct unit for the VIN and information you provided, we will contact you and issue a full refund.

You are responsible for giving us accurate information about the VIN, vehicle configuration, engine, drive type, modifications, tuning, tire size, towing, plow use, installer and delivery location.

2. Payment, pricing and sales tax

Payment is made to Integrity Transmission & Drivetrain through Stripe. Stripe shows the transmission price, separately itemized refundable core deposit, freight, applicable sales tax and complete amount before you pay. Integrity does not receive your full card number.

Online checkout currently processes taxable retail purchases. It does not accept resale or tax-exemption certificates. Stripe calculates and collects tax where Integrity has an active obligation to collect it.

Integrity does not place the remanufacturer order until Stripe confirms payment and our final fitment review is complete. A payment that remains pending is not treated as cleared.

3. Availability, build time and delivery

Inventory and build estimates can change. An “in stock” result means a finished unit was shown when the website checked; it is not a reservation until payment and final review are complete. A build estimate is separate from carrier transit time.

Freight is calculated from the delivery address and location type you enter. Residential service, liftgate service, a commercial address without a dock, or changing the destination can change the price. If the address changes, the freight rate must be recalculated before the order can proceed.

If you choose prepaid core-return freight, the displayed freight line includes outbound delivery and one return shipment for the core. If you choose outbound delivery only, you are responsible for arranging and paying for the core return.

Inspect the shipment when it arrives. Note visible damage with the carrier before signing, preserve the packaging, take clear photographs and contact Integrity promptly.

4. Refundable core deposit

The core deposit is collected with the order. To qualify for a full core refund, return the correct, complete original transmission in the supplied reusable container within 30 days after delivery and follow the return instructions provided with the order.

The remanufacturer must receive, process and accept the core before Integrity releases the refund. A wrong, incomplete, disassembled, damaged or late core may receive reduced or no credit. If you told us that the original transmission is missing, incomplete or damaged, do not assume the deposit will be refundable until we confirm eligibility in writing.

When a core is accepted, Integrity refunds the approved core amount and adjusts the sales tax associated with that refunded amount when applicable. Refund timing after approval depends on Stripe and the customer’s bank or card issuer.

5. Installation, programming and warranty

Nationwide orders must be installed by a qualified repair facility unless Integrity has separately confirmed a Springfield-area coordinated installation. The installer must follow all written installation, cooler service, fluid, programming, relearn, documentation and warranty requirements supplied with the unit.

Warranty length and included components vary by package and application. The package selected in checkout controls; general website descriptions do not replace the written warranty and installation requirements delivered with the transmission.

Damage caused by incorrect installation, contamination, inadequate cooling, improper fluid, missing programming or relearn, vehicle modifications, misuse or failure to follow the written requirements may not be covered.

6. Changes, cancellations, returns and refunds

Contact Integrity immediately if any order information is wrong. We will correct the order before the remanufacturer order is placed whenever possible. Once a unit has entered production, been specially configured or shipped, cancellation or return may be limited by the written terms for that unit.

Packages that require personal approval or are identified as non-returnable are not eligible for instant online checkout. If the website accepts payment but we discover that the selected unit cannot be supplied, is discontinued or is not the correct fit, Integrity will issue a full refund to the original payment method.

A customer-requested return after a correct unit has been ordered or shipped is different from a core return and may require written authorization. Applicable freight, restocking or other return conditions will be disclosed before an authorized return proceeds.
