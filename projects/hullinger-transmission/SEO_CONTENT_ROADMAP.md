# Integrity SEO Content Roadmap

Updated: September 2, 2026

## Objective

Grow qualified search traffic for Integrity Transmission & Drivetrain without creating thin location pages, duplicating existing service pages, or publishing claims that cannot be supported by real business evidence.

The content program has three jobs:

1. Improve the pages Google is already testing for Springfield-area service searches.
2. Build national demand around remanufactured transmission research and VIN-assisted quotes.
3. Publish useful symptom and case-study resources that can earn links and convert readers into calls or quote requests.

## Case-Study Selection Rules

A case study is publishable only when Integrity can document:

- the real vehicle and general configuration;
- the customer's original complaint;
- diagnostic evidence such as codes, fluid/pan findings, scan data, or teardown photos;
- the options discussed and why the final repair, rebuild, or reman solution was selected;
- installation, programming, cooler, converter, and warranty requirements relevant to the outcome;
- a real post-service result with a stated follow-up interval; and
- customer permission for any identifiable vehicle, testimonial, or images.

Never invent a customer, diagnosis, price, turnaround time, mileage result, or testimonial. Do not publish a full VIN, plate, customer name, address, phone number, or unredacted repair order.

## Content Quality Standard

Every new guide should:

- answer one distinct intent instead of competing with an existing service page;
- identify what the symptom can and cannot prove;
- explain what evidence a driver or repair shop should collect;
- state when driving should stop;
- distinguish diagnosis, repair, rebuild, and remanufactured replacement;
- include original technical review and, when available, original photos;
- link naturally to the relevant service, warranty, transmission, or reman page;
- use a unique title, description, H1, canonical, hero image, and structured data; and
- end with a useful next step rather than a generic sales pitch.

## Avoiding Keyword Cannibalization

- Keep Springfield service intent on the existing service pages.
- Keep national product/quote intent on `/reman-transmissions`.
- Keep education and symptom intent in `/guides/`.
- Keep transmission-family expertise in `/transmissions/`.
- A guide may link to a service page, but it should not repeat the service page's title, H1, or core sales copy.
- Do not create near-identical pages for Springfield, Ozark, Nixa, Republic, and other cities. Strengthen the service-area page and real local proof instead.

## Measurement Plan

Review monthly, but judge new content over a minimum 8–12 week window unless a technical issue appears sooner.

Track:

- indexed/not indexed status;
- impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position by page and query;
- reman quote form starts and submissions;
- calls and text clicks from guide and reman pages;
- qualified lead rate, quote rate, and completed-order rate;
- links and referring domains earned by each asset; and
- real customer questions that should change or expand the content queue.

Use the scheduled monthly Integrity SEO review as the checkpoint. Do not judge success by rankings alone; qualified quote requests and completed work are the business outcomes.
