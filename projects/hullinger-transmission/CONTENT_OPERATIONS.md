# Integrity Content Operations

Updated: September 21, 2026

## Purpose

Integrity publishes the complete original resource on `https://integritydrivetrain.com/guides`. That page is the canonical source and the permanent destination for search, internal links and qualified quote traffic. YouTube, Shorts, Facebook, Google Business Profile and email adapt the material for their audiences and send interested people back to the guide or the relevant VIN quote page.

This record tracks the work without inventing customer jobs, supplier claims, prices, inventory, testimonials or technical results. A draft may contain a clearly marked placeholder internally, but no placeholder may enter the public website, structured data, social channel, customer message or production Office database.

## Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning | Publication use |
|---|---|---|
| A — documented firsthand | Integrity job record, original media, measurement, invoice or test result | May support an attributed case study after privacy and permission review |
| B — verified external | OEM, government, supplier, ATSG or other authoritative documentation | May support factual technical or commercial statements within its scope |
| C — expert judgment | William's clearly identified experience, preference or recommendation | Publish as judgment, not a universal specification or guarantee |
| D — placeholder | Drafting aid, missing evidence or planned asset | Internal only; blocks publication |

## One guide becomes a distribution package

| Channel | Deliverable | Primary job |
|---|---|---|
| Integrity website | Complete guide with sources, related pages and CTA | Permanent search authority and conversion |
| YouTube | One 6–12 minute explanation | Demonstrate expertise and answer the full question naturally |
| Shorts | Two or three 20–60 second answers | Reach one misconception, symptom or mistake at a time |
| Facebook | Two native posts | Start useful discussion and show practical experience |
| Google Business Profile | One concise local-service angle when relevant | Keep the local profile useful and current |
| Email | Optional short excerpt after a consented list exists | Re-engage customers and leads |

## Required content record

Every topic retains:

- working title, slug, cluster and product family;
- target question, search intent and related sales/service page;
- evidence grade, source list and technical reviewer;
- original-media provenance and customer permission when applicable;
- status, owner, due date, blockers and approval history;
- canonical URL and channel-variant URLs;
- published, updated, review and refresh dates;
- UTM-tagged distribution links; and
- impressions, position, CTR, engaged visits, product clicks, VIN-quote starts, quote submissions and attributable revenue when available.

Statuses move in this order:

`Idea → Evidence Needed → Ready to Draft → Technical Review → Ready to Publish → Published → Distributed → Performance Review → Refresh Due`

## Weekly workflow

1. Choose a topic using Search Console opportunity, purchase intent, real expertise, product readiness and evidence risk.
2. Build a one-page brief with the exact question, audience, decision, sources, media needs, related product page and CTA.
3. Record a 10–20 minute explanation from William or collect written technical notes.
4. Assemble the evidence packet and label each claim A, B, C or D.
5. Draft the guide with the direct answer first, meaningful limitations, internal links and a useful next action.
6. Complete technical, privacy, commercial-terms and media-rights review.
7. Publish the canonical guide, regenerate the sitemap, run the full test suite and release it.
8. Produce the long video, short clips, Facebook posts and any legitimate local Google Business Profile angle.
9. Record each publication URL and tagged link.
10. Review search and conversion results after 8–12 weeks, then revise the title, introduction, internal links, CTA or content when the evidence supports a change.

## Topic scoring

Score each candidate from 0–3 in each category.

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Purchase intent | ×3 |
| Existing Search Console opportunity | ×3 |
| William's firsthand expertise | ×2 |
| Verified product/supplier readiness | ×2 |
| Original photos or job evidence | ×1 |
| Internal-link value | ×1 |
| Unverified-claim risk | subtract ×3 |

Queries already receiving impressions in positions 5–20 normally move ahead of speculative topics.

## Long-video outline

1. State the exact question and direct answer.
2. Explain which vehicles, units or buyers the answer applies to.
3. Show the component, identifier, document or evidence.
4. Explain the common misunderstanding or failure in ordinary shop language.
5. Compare repair, rebuild, reman, fitment or installation choices as relevant.
6. State the important limitations and what must be verified for the exact VIN.
7. Direct the viewer to the complete guide or VIN quote.

## Short-video outline

- Open with one direct question or misconception in the first two seconds.
- Show the relevant component, photograph or simple on-screen label.
- Explain one useful fact only.
- End with a practical next step; do not use generic engagement bait.
