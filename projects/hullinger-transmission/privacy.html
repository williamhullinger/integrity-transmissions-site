Privacy Policy

How Integrity Transmission & Drivetrain handles information submitted for quotes, orders, service and support.

Effective September 3, 2026

On this page Information We Collect How We Use It When We Share It Payments Retention & Security Your Choices

1. Information we collect

When you request a quote, schedule service or order a remanufactured transmission, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, vehicle identification number (VIN), vehicle details, mileage, transmission information, installer and programming information, vehicle use or modifications, diagnostic details and messages you send us.

Our website and service providers may also record basic technical information needed to operate and protect the site, such as IP address, browser type, device information, request time, referring page, error details and security or fraud signals.

2. How we use information

We use information to identify the correct transmission, prepare and recheck pricing, calculate freight and tax, process payment, review fitment and payment risk, arrange fulfillment, coordinate core returns, provide warranty or customer support, prevent fraud, keep required business records and communicate about your request or order.

We may use de-identified or aggregated operational information to understand which services are requested and improve the website. We do not send VINs, contact details or delivery addresses to advertising analytics.

3. When we share information

We share only the information reasonably needed with companies that help us provide the requested service. These can include Stripe for payment and tax processing, website hosting and form providers, the remanufacturer or fulfillment partner, freight carriers, a qualified installer when requested, email or communication providers, and professional advisers.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect customers or the business, to investigate fraud or security issues, or as part of a business transfer. We do not sell personal information.

4. Payment information

Online payment is handled through Stripe Checkout. Integrity receives payment status, totals, tax, customer and order details needed to process the order, but does not receive your full payment-card number. Stripe handles payment information under its own privacy and security practices.

5. Retention and security

We retain quote, order, payment, tax, core-return, warranty and communication records for as long as reasonably needed to complete the transaction, provide support, resolve disputes and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations. Information that is no longer needed is deleted, anonymized or retained only where required.

We use administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information and the services involved. No website, email or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not send payment-card numbers, passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information in a message.

6. Your choices and questions

You may ask us to correct inaccurate contact or vehicle information, request a copy of information associated with your order, or ask us to delete information that we are not required to retain. Some records must be kept for payment, tax, fraud-prevention, warranty or legal reasons.

This website and its online ordering service are intended for adults. We may update this policy as the ordering system or legal requirements change; the effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact Integrity

For a privacy question or request, call (417) 815-3315 or email info@integritydrivetrain.com. Please do not include a payment-card number in your message.
