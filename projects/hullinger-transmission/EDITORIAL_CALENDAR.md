# Integrity Powertrain Editorial Calendar

Updated: September 21, 2026

## Publishing model

The complete, original resource publishes first on `https://integritydrivetrain.com/guides`. That website page is the canonical source. A published guide may then be adapted into a YouTube explanation, short vertical videos, a Facebook post, a locally relevant Google Business Profile post and an email excerpt. Every adaptation should link back to the complete guide instead of copying the entire article to another website.

Statuses move in this order:

`Idea → Evidence Needed → Ready to Draft → Technical Review → Ready to Publish → Published → Refresh Due`

## Repurposing checklist

For each published guide:

- [ ] Add one useful, non-sales excerpt to Facebook with the canonical link.
- [ ] Record one 5–10 minute shop or bench explanation.
- [ ] Cut two or three 30–60 second clips, each answering one question.
- [ ] Add the full video to YouTube with the guide link in the description.
- [ ] Embed the full video in the guide when it adds information rather than decoration.
- [ ] Prepare a Google Business Profile post only when the topic has a genuine local-service angle.
- [ ] Add a short email-digest entry when a verified mailing list and consent process exist.
- [ ] Pitch a separately written version to a relevant trade or partner publication when it offers original evidence.
- [ ] Record publication URLs and UTM-tagged distribution links.
- [ ] Review search impressions, CTR, engagement and qualified actions after 8–12 weeks.

## Required article record

Every new item should retain:

- slug and working title;
- category and powertrain system;
- transmission/engine/transfer-case family when relevant;
- intended audience and search intent;
- evidence level and source list;
- author and technical reviewer;
- photo/video provenance and permission;
- status, publication date, update date and refresh date;
- related service/product page and primary call to action; and
- performance notes after the first review window.

## Publication rules

- Do not invent a vehicle, job, diagnosis, outcome, mileage, customer quote or photograph.
- Do not publish compatibility, power capacity, availability, price, warranty or supplier-process claims without current evidence.
- A representative illustration must be labeled as representative when a reader could mistake it for the exact product or job.
- Remove full VINs, license plates, names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, repair-order numbers, payment data and revealing image metadata.
- Use one distinct search intent per page; do not create thin city variants or near-duplicate family pages.
- The written quote and unit-specific warranty control commercial terms even when a guide explains the general process.
